Cattle Behaviour Analysis

I worked on an image-processing-based cattle behavioural analysis system. The system is intended to:

  • Identify individual cows
  • Log their movements and behavior
  • Analyze cattle behavior
  • Detect abnormal behavior
  • Automatically generate alerts

An additional important part is integrating different databases into a single reference system, including information such as:

  • Milking database
  • Veterinary database
  • Cowshed/farm database

The purpose is to provide a more integrated management view rather than treating each database independently. The project is progressing incrementally.

I focused on the research idea, problem definition, and overall system design. The implementation was carried out substantially in collaboration with another developer.
