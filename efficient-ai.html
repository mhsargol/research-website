Efficient AI & Intelligent Computing

Current Research

More recently, my research has focused on AI and efficient computing. I am currently developing research around efficient neural-network inference.

The broad goal is to make trained neural networks execute more efficiently, particularly in terms of:

  • Memory access
  • Execution time
  • Energy consumption
  • Implementation efficiency
  • Automated inference-code generation
  • Less performance-demanding processor requirements in the inference phase

Research Direction

The work includes concepts such as:

  • Memory-aware optimization
  • Parameter quantization
  • Architecture-aware transformation
  • Automated inference-code generation
  • Energy-efficiency considerations

A conceptual idea I find particularly important is: preserve the information but change its presentation — and reorganize the surrounding computation around that representation. This has a conceptual relationship with my fault-tolerant MLP neural network and CGRA research.

Current Status

Some experimental results have already been obtained, while additional experiments and evaluations are being gathered. The first paper from this research is currently being prepared and is expected to be submitted within the coming months.

The "Prepare" Concept (Private)

A proposed lifecycle for neural networks is: Training → Prepare → Inference.

However, "Prepare" is currently private. I do not want to publicly present "Prepare" as an established research contribution until the first paper has been sufficiently developed and submitted/published.
